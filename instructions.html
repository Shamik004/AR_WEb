How to Use the AR Drawing App

1. Select a Bengali Character: Choose a Bengali character from the data collection page to practice drawing.

2. Start Drawing with Hand Gestures: Use your index finger to draw the selected Bengali character in the air. The app will track your finger movements and create a drawing on the screen.

3. Pause Drawing: To pause the drawing, bring your index finger and thumb closer together. This gesture will temporarily stop the drawing function.

4. Resume Drawing: Separate your index finger and thumb to resume drawing with your index finger.

5. Control Buttons: Use the "Clear" button to erase your current drawing and start over. Use the "Save" button to save your handwritten character data.

6. Data Collection: Practice drawing the Bengali character multiple times to collect quality handwriting data for machine learning purposes.

Tips for Best Results:
• Keep your hand well-lit and visible to the camera
• Draw at a moderate speed for better tracking
• Make sure your background is not too cluttered
• Position yourself at arm's length from the camera
